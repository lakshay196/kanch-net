0. ONE SENTENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Kanch-Net is a two-home app where Firozabad glass families post small stock,
buyers post one clear demand, matching piles meet by specification first and
nearness second, a demo piece is accepted in chat before money moves into a
trust pool, and families are paid only for pieces that pass quality check.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. WHY THIS EXISTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A wholesale buyer wants one large, consistent lot — for the demo story,
10,000 red glass bangles, size 2–6, plain glossy, Grade B (order ORD-001).

No single home can supply that alone. Twenty homes can — but only if:

  • incompatible piles are kept out (wrong colour, size, grade),
  • nearer matching homes are preferred after the spec fits,
  • the buyer sees a real demo piece before locking a large quantity,
  • money does not evaporate into udhaar after the glass leaves the house,
  • rejected stock is not thrown away — it stays available for another buyer.

Kanch-Net is the cooperative’s thin operating screen for that story.
It does not replace the collector’s hands, the mandi’s eyes, or a bank.
It makes the rules visible so both sides can trust the same notebook.

Heritage note (honest): Firozabad glass is living craft and livelihood.
A craft survives when the people who carry the skill can earn reliably.
This product is economic infrastructure for that craft — not a decorative
“heritage skin” on a generic catalogue.

2. PRODUCT FACTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FACT 1 — Two interfaces after one login
  Everyone starts on the same login page.
  Phone + code (1234) decides who you are.
  Artisan login opens the artisan home.
  Buyer login opens the buyer home.
  Collector and coordinator have staff screens (pickup / matching / money).
  The main pitch is artisan home ↔ buyer home.

FACT 2 — Glass bangles are live; pottery and textile are locked
  On the artisan home, craft is a dropdown:
    • Bangle makers — live
    • Pottery — labelled (making in process)
    • Textile — labelled (making in process)
  Selecting pottery or textile does not unlock posting for this demo.

FACT 3 — Cross feeds (the basic marketplace loop)
  Artisan posts stock: quantity, quality (grade), colour (and size for glass).
  That stock appears on the buyer feed.
  Buyer posts demand: quantity, quality, colour, size.
  That demand appears on the artisan feed.
  Same browser, shared local store — so logout → login as the other role
  still sees the posts in this prototype.

FACT 4 — Spec first, then nearest
  A pile may join a demand only if product, size, colour, finish, and
  minimum grade rules pass (deterministic rules — not AI).
  Among piles that pass, the buyer feed sorts nearer localities first
  (demo kilometres from Firozabad mandi — not live GPS tracking).
  Wrong colour (e.g. blue when the buyer wants red) stays OUT with a
  plain-language reason.

FACT 5 — Demo piece in chat before large-order money
  Before Confirm on a large quantity, the artisan sends a demo piece in
  the in-app chat (colour, small qty, optional photo).
  Buyer Accept → demand quantity locks (cannot shrink).
  Buyer Reject → pile stays in the pool for another buyer; stock is not wasted.
  Confirm stays closed until a demo is accepted.

FACT 6 — Money pool against udhaar (simulated ledger, not a bank)
  After demo Accept:
    1) Buyer pays booking (~25% of lot value) — filters time-passers.
    2) Buyer Confirm puts the remaining amount into the in-app money pool.
    3) Collector records QC (collected / good / broken).
    4) Released rupees follow accepted pieces only (minus co-op fee %).
    5) Rejected quantity’s buyer-side value returns conceptually to the buyer.
  No real UPI. No GPay-looking Pay button. No Aadhaar. Judges are told:
  this is demo money that shows the trust logic.

FACT 7 — Map for nearness
  /map shows the mandi and household localities as pins (Leaflet + OSM,
  assets vendored in the app). List under the map ranks nearer places first.
  Map does not override specification. Spec match still wins; distance ranks
  among matches and helps the “people near you” story.

FACT 8 — Producer reliability score
  Shown on the artisan bio and on buyer stock rows.
  Seed scores exist when there is no QC yet (Family 1 high; Family 3 lower).
  After QC: score ≈ accepted ÷ (accepted + rejected) × 100.
  Purpose: buyer trust + artisan motivation for quality.

FACT 9 — Prototype boundary (live build)
  Next.js front-end, starter JSON + browser localStorage.
  No FastAPI backend, no shared cloud database between phones,
  no live payments partner, no QR claim in this build.
  GST / Aadhaar / mandatory unit–firm location capture are not live yet;
  they are locked for the next MVP cut (see Section 11).
  Chrome Web Speech may assist artisan entry when the browser allows it;
  it is assistive, not a trained dialect ASR product.

6. MATCHING RULES (THE “ENGINE” — HONEST NAME)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This is not machine learning.
It is a short, auditable checklist in code (lib/match.ts):

  1. Same product family (glass bangle for the live path)
  2. Same size band
  3. Same colour family
  4. Compatible finish
  5. Pile grade meets or beats the buyer’s minimum grade

First failure wins the OUT reason.
IN piles are then ordered by demo distance from Firozabad mandi.

Staff matching page (/matching) shows the same IN / OUT idea in plain
Hindi + English for the coordinator demo.

7. FEATURE DEPTH — THE FOUR TRUST LAYERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

These four layers sit on top of the basic two-home feeds.
They do not replace the feeds; they make the feeds safe enough to pitch.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 Chat + demo piece (risk before volume)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem solved
  A buyer who rejects a large unseen lot leaves the artisan with dead stock
  and lost time. A demo first is how humans already trade; the app only
  makes that step unskippable before Confirm.

Flow in the product
  Artisan opens chat on a demand (with their pile).
  Sends demo: colour, small quantity, optional photo.
  Buyer Accept → quantity locked for that demand; booking/confirm unlock path.
  Buyer Reject → pile remains available; another buyer can still take it.
  “No stock wasted” is a product promise of pooling, not a magic same-day sale.

What judges should hear
  “We force a sample handshake before the big number becomes money.”

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.2 Money pool (the anti-udhaar spine)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem solved
  Udhaar — payment delayed or denied after goods leave the home — is the
  wound artisans name most often. A marketplace that only matches SKUs but
  leaves payment as a handshake outside the system has not finished the job.

What the prototype proves (logic, not banking licence)
  Booking (~25%) — skin in the game; reduces idle enquiries.
  Confirm → remaining value sits in the visible pool.
  QC gate — release follows accepted pieces only.
  Reject path — buyer-side returned amount for rejected quantity.
  Family payout lines use accepted × artisan unit rate, minus co-op fee %.

Rates live in starter data (buyer unit price, artisan unit price,
booking percent, co-op fee percent). The UI says Money / पैसा — not a fake
GPay. Verbal honesty to judges: simulated ledger.

What judges should hear
  “We are not a bank. We are a transparent notebook that only opens the
   artisan’s share after quality, and returns what failed.”

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.3 Map + distance preference (meet the nearest fit)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem solved
  Even correct colour piles should not ignore geography. Collection cost,
  trust, and time all shrink when matching homes are nearby.

What the product does
  Locality pins around the mandi; list sorted by demo km.
  Buyer IN feed sorts matching piles by that same nearness.
  Spec still vetoes a nearer wrong pile. Distance never promotes a mismatch.

What judges should hear
  “First the glass must match. Then the shortest road wins.”

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.4 Producer reliability score (trust that compounds)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem solved
  Buyers fear unknown homes. Artisans with careful QC deserve a visible edge.
  A score on the bio and on each proposal row makes quality socially legible.

What the product does
  Badge on artisan home and buyer stock rows.
  Seed scores until QC exists; then QC ratio drives the number.
  Family 1 starts strong; Family 3 starts lower (blue / reject story).

What judges should hear
  “Reputation is earned in accepted pieces, not in profile poetry.”

8. COLLECTOR AND MONEY STAFF PATH
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Collector (/pickup) — three large steps
  1. Which pile (family + colour + declared pieces)
  2. Numbers: collected / good / broken
  3. Save — QC writes into the shared collector map so pool + score refresh

Money (/money)

  Released list: families paid on accepted pieces

This closes the loop from feed → demo → booking → confirm → QC → release.

11. MVP ADDITIONS (LOCKED — NOT LIVE YET)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

These three onboarding facts are mandatory for the next MVP cut.
They are product requirements for trust and collection geography.
They are not in the current Vercel / localhost demo screens.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11.1 GST number — buyer verification
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Who
  Wholesale / firm buyers (buyer role).

What
  Buyer must enter GSTIN during onboarding or before raising a demand
  that can enter the money-pool path.

Why
  Separates a real trading firm from a time-passer phone.
  Gives the cooperative a verifiable commercial identity for the lot.
  Supports later invoice / settlement paperwork with a licensed partner.

MVP rules
  • Field is required for buyer accounts that post demand.
  • Format check for GSTIN pattern in the UI (basic validation).
  • Store as firm identity on the buyer profile; show masked on public feeds
    if shown at all (full number for cooperative staff only).
  • Live demo today: not collected. Pitch line: “Next MVP — GST on buyer.”

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11.2 Aadhaar number — artisan identity
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Who
  Household artisans (artisan role).

What
  Artisan must register Aadhaar (or Aadhaar-linked verification via an
  authorised partner flow) as part of joining the cooperative path.

Why
  One household = one accountable producer for batch ownership and payout.
  Reduces duplicate / ghost seller accounts on the artisan feed.
  Ties reliability score and settlement lines to a real person/household.

MVP rules (honest + lawful)
  • Required at artisan onboarding before stock can enter a paid lot.
  • Prefer OTP / partner KYC verification over storing full number in plain
    text on a student laptop; if stored in prototype, mask on screen
    (show last 4 only) and never print full Aadhaar on buyer feeds.
  • Cooperative staff may see verification status: Verified / Pending.
  • Live demo today: not collected. Pitch line: “Next MVP — Aadhaar for
    artisan join; we verify, we do not splash the number on the bazaar feed.”

Legal posture for judges
  Kanch-Net is not claiming UIDAI API access in the student build.
  MVP means the product requires artisan identity; implementation may use
  an authorised verification partner when the cooperative goes live.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11.3 Location — firm location (buyer) + manufacturing unit (artisan)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Who / what (both sides — must enter)

  Buyer
    Firm location (office / godown / mandi desk where the lot is received
    or managed). Mandatory on buyer profile.

  Artisan
    Manufacturing unit location — jahaan bhi unit ho, wahi ki location.
    Not “somewhere in Firozabad” as free vibes: a concrete place the
    collector can reach. Mandatory before stock can be offered into a
    matched lot.

Why
  Nearness ranking and the map only work if both sides declare where they
  actually are. Collection cost, trust visits, and “shortest road after
  spec match” all depend on real unit / firm pins.
  Without unit location, distance preference is theatre.

MVP rules
  • Required fields on both onboarding forms.
  • Capture locality + pin (map pick or typed address + geo).
  • Artisan manufacturing unit location feeds /map pins and km sort.
  • Buyer firm location anchors the hub side of distance (or sits beside
    the shared mandi hub if the firm receives at mandi).
  • Cannot post stock (artisan) or raise a pool-eligible demand (buyer)
    until location is saved.
  • Live demo today: seed localities in data (Ramnagar, Suhag Nagar, …)
    stand in for this. Pitch line: “Next MVP — every artisan types their
    unit location; every buyer types firm location. No blank geography.”

14. CLOSING PRINCIPLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Kanch-Net is simple on purpose.

One door.
Two homes.
Stock and demand that see each other.
Match the glass, then shorten the road.
Show a piece before you lock a thousand.
Hold money in a clear pool until quality says yes.
Let a family’s score rise only when their glass does.

Then harden the door:
  buyers with GST and a firm place,
  artisans with verified identity and a real unit on the map.

That is the product — a clickable notebook for a craft city, with a clear
next MVP that makes every account and every kilometre accountable.
