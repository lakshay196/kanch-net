--------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE ARE MAKING
--------------------------------------------------

20 families. Each made a small pile of glass bangles at home.
A shop wants 10,000 bangles that are ALL red, size 2-6, same shiny look.

Our app:
- families tell the phone what they made
- the app keeps matching piles and says why a pile is rejected
- a collector picks up the goods
- a checker says how many pieces are actually good
- a fake money page shows what each family would get

We are NOT making real payments, Amazon, or Aadhaar.
